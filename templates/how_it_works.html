How this works

A short guide — no jargon.
  1. What TrackTect does

    You tell it a company name. It finds their website and public pages, then watches for meaningful changes — like new products, pricing, campaigns, or big news.

  2. What “tracking” means

    Once a company is on your list, TrackTect checks them on a schedule (and whenever you tap Check now). You don’t need to keep searching yourself.

  3. How you’ll know something changed

    Updates show up on Overview and on each company’s page. Important ones also appear in your alert bell. The PM Briefing page gathers everything section by section.

  4. Searches and more access

    Each new lookup or check uses one of your free searches. Looking at past results is always free. If you run out, ask an admin to give you more.
